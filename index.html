    How to Play

    Rules

    • Every 25 kills without dying raises the multiplier: x2 at 25, then 50, 100, 200, 400, 800, 1,200, 1,600 and x10 at 2,000. Dying resets it.
    • You start with 3 bombs and 3 spare ships (max 9 each); extra ship every 75,000, extra bomb every 100,000.
    • Every 10,000 points your gun may change.
    • An awake Gravity Well pulls you in and bends your bullets away.
    • A bomb clears the arena but scores nothing. Losing a ship clears it too.
    • Touching anything is fatal, including the walls of a Gravity Well and a Snake's tail.
