# TokenTrainer

A Duolingo-style trainer for crypto trading, played on **real historical token data** (Binance spot candles).
Each lesson replays a real market event candle by candle; you make a decision at each checkpoint and get graded,
detailed feedback on it.

Open `index.html` in a browser — no build step, no server, works offline (`npm start` serves it on :8000 if you prefer).

## How it plays

- **Learning path** — 4 units × 3 lessons, unlocked in order, 1–3 stars each; guidebook per unit.
- **Lessons** — 7–10 decisions on real candles (daily, 4-hour or hourly), plus chart-reading and risk-math quizzes.
  Headlines that were public at the time appear as the chart reaches their date.
- **Moves** — Hold · Buy small (+25% of account) · Buy big (+50%) · All in · Trim half · Sell all, each with an optional
  protective stop (−5/−10/−20% or a 10% trailing stop). Spot only: no leverage, no shorts.
- **Realistic fills** — trades at the candle close; 0.1% fee; slippage that grows with volatility; stops fill at the
  trigger, at the open on a gap, and further below the trigger in crash candles.
- **No rewind** — every answer is committed the moment you press Check and saved; reloading resumes *after* it.
  Quitting ends the attempt. You can **replay the whole scenario** from the start as often as you like.
- **Hearts** — 5 per lesson. Lost on wrong quiz answers and on Mistakes/Blunders *with poor process* (bad decisions,
  not bad luck). Out of hearts, or the account below 40% of start, ends the lesson.
- **XP, daily goal, streak, achievements** — kept in the browser (localStorage). Settings: sound, theme, blind mode.
- **Practice** — a random real window from 10 major tokens (2019–2026), blind: no name, dates or news until the reveal.

## Feedback

Each decision gets a grade (Excellent · Good · Inaccuracy · Mistake · Blunder) from two scores:

- **Process (65%)** — judged only on what was knowable at that close: trend (price vs rising/falling 20- and 50-bar
  averages), momentum (RSI, distance from the average), volatility (ATR), capitulation signs (volume spikes after
  drops), position size, risk per trade (size × stop distance vs the 1–2% rule), stop placement vs normal noise,
  flip-flopping, and the scenario coach's read of key moments.
- **Outcome (35%)** — what actually happened over the next candles, compared with every other choice you could have
  made (shown in a hindsight table).

The feedback sheet explains both, calls out "right process, bad luck" and "lucky gamble" cases, and shows stop
fills. Every lesson ends with a reveal of the real event and its takeaway.

## Data

`data/market-data.js` is generated by `scripts/fetch_data.py` from Binance's public archive
(`data.binance.vision`, monthly kline CSVs; no API key). Rebuild with `npm run data` (or `--only <key>` for one
dataset). Scenario content lives in `js/scenarios.js`, keyed to the dataset names in the fetch script.

## Code

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `js/engine.js` | Sessions, execution, stops, grading, quizzes, summary — pure logic, no DOM |
| `js/indicators.js` | SMA, RSI, ATR |
| `js/scenarios.js` | Units, lessons, headlines, coach notes, reveals |
| `js/chart.js` | Canvas candlestick chart |
| `js/store.js` | Progress, streak, achievements, the in-progress attempt |
| `js/app.js` | UI |

`npm test` runs the engine tests; `npm run calibrate` prints how simple strategies grade across all lessons.
