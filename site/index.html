Questions

Will it delete anything I need?

Sift removes only files from a fixed catalogue of macOS bookkeeping files, and never in system folders, apps, ~/Library, Time Machine or read-only disks. A disk's Trash and in-progress saves are only offered in a Sweep, with what removing them means.

Why do .DS_Store files keep coming back without it?

Finder writes one into every folder you open to remember its view, and ._ files whenever a Mac copies to a disk that cannot store Mac metadata. Sift removes each the moment it is written, and turns on Finder's own switches for not writing them on network and USB disks.

What happens when the trial ends?

Cleaning by itself and Finder views pause; views already applied stay. Sweep keeps working, free. Enter a key at any time to turn Pro back on.

How many Macs can I use it on?

Your key works on your own Macs; Deactivate on This Mac moves it to a new one.

How do I get my license key?
