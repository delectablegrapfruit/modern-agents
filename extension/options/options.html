Scroll to Scrub

Hover a video and scroll sideways to scrub through it.

Scrubbing

s per 100 px

Seconds of video per 100 px of wheel or trackpad movement, whatever the video's length. Every bit of movement counts; there is no dead zone and no snapping.

Axis

Which wheel direction scrubs. With Horizontal only, vertical scrolling over a video still scrolls the page.

Shift + wheel

For mice without a tilt or thumb wheel.

Direction

Modifier keys

Useful on sites with sideways-scrolling carousels that contain videos.

Scrubs at 1/10 speed while held, for landing on an exact frame.

Playback

While scrubbing

The video stays on the frame you stopped on for as long as the pointer rests on it. Clicking or pressing a key hands control back to the player without restarting playback.

Overlay

Off by default: the site's own player already shows the position.

Sites

One hostname per line. Subdomains are included, and so are players embedded from other sites (a YouTube iframe on a disabled site stays off).

Keyboard shortcut

Toggle on / off

Alt + Shift + S by default.

Undo last scrub

Alt + Shift + Z by default (also in the toolbar popup).

Puts the video back where it was before the last scrub, playing if it was playing. Press again to redo. Change the shortcuts.
